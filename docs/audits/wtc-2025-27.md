# World Test Championship 2025-27: rules, data checks and backtest

Recorded on 21-22 November 2026 when the league was added (`leagues/wtc-2025-27.json`, `wtc.py`, `wtc_model.py`).

## Rules, as encoded

Source of truth: the [ICC World Test Championship 2025-2027 Playing Conditions](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/xgmt8r5r41onkqbb5nxv.pdf) (effective June 2025), read in full for clauses 6.4, 12.9 and 16. The [Competition Regulations](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/doztximb3sxhwcc4oo3f.pdf) (effective 11 April 2025) add forfeits, cancellations and the drawn final.

| Case | Rule | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teams and schedule | Nine teams (AUS, BAN, ENG, IND, NZ, PAK, SA, SL, WI); six series each, three at home and three away, of two to five Tests | PC 16.9 |
| Points | Win 12, tie 6, draw 4, loss 0 | PC 16.10.1, Regs 9.1 |
| Abandoned Test | Classified as a draw (4 points each); if the pitch or outfield is rated unfit, the visitors get a win's points and the hosts none, still a draw in the records | PC 16.10.2, Regs 9.2; a pitch that cannot be repaired is "abandoned as a draw" (PC 6.4) |
| Match awarded | A side that concedes or refuses to play loses; the other side wins | PC 16.2 |
| Forfeit | A Test not played through a party's unacceptable non-compliance counts as a loss for it and a win for the other side | Regs 8.1 |
| Cancelled (accepted reason) | No points, and the Test leaves the percentage altogether | PC 16.12.1, Regs 8.2 and 9.5 |
| Over rate | One point off for every full over short | PC 12.9, 16.11; Regs 9.3 |
| Ranking | Percentage of points: (points won less deductions) / (12 x Tests played) | PC 16.12.1, Regs 9.4 |
| Tie-breakers | Series wins, then away PCT (away Tests only, deductions in them included), then the ICC Test ranking at the end of the window | PC 16.12.2-4, Regs 9.6 |
| Window and ranking date | The Playing Conditions say Friday 30 April 2027; the Regulations say the window ends in March and take the ranking on 1 April 2027. The last Tests end in March, so only the ranking date differs. The config uses 30 April and the page names both. | PC 16.12.4, 16.13.1; Regs 1.2, 9.6 |
| Final | The top two; The Oval, 9-13 June 2027 ([ICC, 23 July 2026](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/venue-and-dates-for-world-test-championship-2027-final-revealed)); a reserve day "to be used if net playing time is lost", date not yet published; full final playing conditions due early 2027 | PC 16.13, Regs 10.1-10.3 |
| Drawn final | A drawn, tied or abandoned final makes the two teams joint champions | Regs 10.4 |

PCT ties are compared exactly (as fractions), not on the two rounded decimals the table shows.

## Schedule notes

- 27 series and 71 Tests, as in the Competition Regulations' schedule; every team has three home and three away series (checked by the config validation).
- Australia v Bangladesh moved from March 2027 to August 2026 (Darwin and Mackay), because of the non-championship 150th anniversary Test at the MCG in March 2027, which the config lists under `otherTests` so it is never matched to the schedule.
- Pakistan v Sri Lanka's second Test moved from Lahore to Rawalpindi (smog).
- No Test has been cancelled, forfeited, awarded, abandoned or played at a neutral ground so far. The engine and its tests cover each case.
- Bangladesh v England, Sri Lanka v South Africa and Pakistan v New Zealand have no published dates; their Tests carry the month only and are marked tentative.

## Exact claims

"Through", "Out", "In control", "Needs help" and "Level at best" come from a search over every remaining result (wins, losses, draws and ties), not from the simulation. `tests/test_wtc.py` compares them with brute-force enumeration on 120 random small championships. On 21 November 2026 the search shows Sri Lanka cannot reach the final on results: their best is 42.36%, South Africa's worst is 42.86%, and Australia and New Zealand, who meet four times, cannot both finish below 42.36%. Wikipedia then marked only Pakistan and West Indies as eliminated. Over-rate deductions still to come are not known, so the page says the claims are on results.

## Backtest

`venv/bin/python scripts/backtest_wtc.py` (22 November 2026, Cricsheet archive of 3 November 2026); full figures and the settings search are in the README's World Test Championship section.

- The script rebuilds the final tables of 2021-23 and 2023-25 from Cricsheet and the ICC's over-rate deductions; both match the ICC's to two decimals (AUS 66.67, IND 58.80 ... BAN 11.11; SA 69.44, AUS 67.54 ... PAK 27.98).
- Match predictions, each made before the Test from the Tests before it: 242 championship Tests of 2019-21, 2021-23, 2023-25 and 2025-27 so far score 0.2048 (ranked probability score) and 0.8786 (log loss), against 0.2379 and 0.9628 for home/draw/away base rates. The model beats the base rates in every cycle.
- Final-place odds at 25%, 50% and 75% of 2021-23 and 2023-25: Brier score 0.0627, against 0.2222 for the table at the time and 0.1728 for a no-skill guess. Rating drift did not help (none 0.0627, 0.1 0.0627, 0.2 0.0632, 0.5 0.0653), so none is used.
- Limits: six season checkpoints make small calibration bins; 2019-21's season odds are not scored because that cycle gave each series 120 points and switched to percentages mid-cycle (its Tests are in the match backtest).
