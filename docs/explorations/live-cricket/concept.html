Match probabilities unavailable

The existing cricket season model estimates qualification scenarios. It does not provide live match win probabilities. No line or percentage is fabricated here.

When available, a 2D 0–100% chart would plot timestamped model estimates tied to deliveries, with exact accessible values, wickets and data gaps. Bookmaker prices would stay in their own view.

Bookmaker oddsUnit: decimal price · external market

Updated: unavailableNo bookmaker feed connected

Market prices unavailable

A future view needs the named bookmaker, market, selection, decimal price, update time and open/suspended/closed state. None is present in this archive.

A reciprocal decimal price is not the Playoff Pulse model chance. Do not combine model estimates and bookmaker prices into one probability bar.
